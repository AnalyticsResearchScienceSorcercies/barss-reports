THE USAID GAP

USAID's $3.085B Haiti portfolio had a Phi (upstream extraction constant) of 0.987... meaning 98.7% of every "aid" dollar was captured by DC-based contractors, consultants, and overhead before it reached a single Haitian. The system didn't fail. It worked exactly as designed... for the extractors.

That system is now gone. The question is not whether something replaces it. The question is whether the replacement is extraction-proof or whether a new set of extractors fills the vacuum.

SAKALA is the extraction-proof answer.

For the first time, we can empirically measure whether extraction-proof development makes people happier. The index, the data infrastructure, and the cooperative are all ready simultaneously.

The Gang Recruitment Window
