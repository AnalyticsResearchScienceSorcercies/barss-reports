Deliverable 1 — Evidence of Indiana Avenue Black Community Economic Wealth Destruction

Built on the foundational model, a multivariate econometric model that combines elements of short-term and long-term economic dynamics will be generated. The model will include:

1. Endogenous variables: Such as output, consumption, investment, wages, profits, and capital stock.

2. Exogenous variables: Including government expenditure, public wages, and business taxes.

3. Simultaneous equations: To capture the complex interactions between economic variables.
