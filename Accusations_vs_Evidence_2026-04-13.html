SHA-256 is a NIST Federal Information Processing Standard (FIPS 180-4). It is the cryptographic hash function mandated by the U.S. federal government for data integrity verification. It is used by:

SHA-256 produces a 256-bit fingerprint of any digital file. If even one bit changes, the hash changes completely. This is not decoration.
