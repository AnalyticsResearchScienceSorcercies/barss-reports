Letter of Intent

Academic Research, Black Happiness Index & Cooperative Data Partnership
Non-Binding Letter of Intent
This Letter of Intent ("LOI") is entered into as of the date last signed below, by and between SAKALA × BARSS Relaunch, a cooperative social enterprise represented by Daniel Tillias and Wesley Bertil (collectively, "SAKALA"), and the Center for Reparations Finance and Practice / Reparations Finance Lab ("RFL"), represented by Enith Williams. SAKALA and RFL are referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."
1. Purpose

RFL shall provide academic research capacity, peer-reviewed publication infrastructure, and the Black Happiness Index (BHI) survey instrument. SAKALA shall provide franchise data, cooperative infrastructure, and dedicated research surcharge funding. Together, the Parties intend to build the "From Extraction to Happiness" research program, demonstrating that extraction-proof cooperative models produce measurable improvements in community wellbeing.

2. Term

This LOI shall remain in effect for a period of five (5) years from the date of execution, automatically renewable for successive two-year periods unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least six (6) months prior to the end of the then-current term. The longer initial term reflects the sustained commitment required for meaningful longitudinal research.

3. RFL Obligations

Design and deploy the Black Happiness Index survey instrument through SAKALA's KoboToolbox infrastructure, ensuring methodological rigor and cross-cultural validity.

Publish two to three (2–3) peer-reviewed papers per year using SAKALA franchise data, cooperative outcomes, and BHI findings.

Develop and lead the "From Extraction to Happiness" research program, establishing the academic framework that connects forensic extraction economics to cooperative wellbeing outcomes.

Present findings at academic conferences and policy forums, including but not limited to venues relevant to reparations, cooperative economics, development finance, and Black diaspora studies.

Develop a documented, publishable Replication Framework enabling other countries and communities to adopt the SAKALA model, as further described in Section 8.

Submit a Ford Foundation Letter of Inquiry ($750,000 over 3 years, BUILD program) with SAKALA as the named implementation partner. If funded, allocation between the Parties shall be governed by a separate budget agreement.

4. SAKALA Obligations

Implement a 1% Research Surcharge on gross franchise revenue, directed to RFL, as further described in Section 5.

Provide access to all franchise data, including KoboToolbox survey results, financial records, and youth outcome metrics, for use in RFL's research program.

Deploy the BHI survey instrument at all active franchise sites in coordination with RFL's research design and data collection protocols.

Acknowledge RFL partnership in all research-related communications, publications, and grant applications.

Support RFL grant applications with operational data, impact metrics, and cooperative performance documentation as reasonably requested.

6. Black Happiness Index
Black Happiness Index — Governance

RFL designs the instrument. SAKALA deploys it. Both Parties co-own the BHI methodology. BHI data is jointly held. Neither Party may publish BHI findings without co-authorship by the other Party.

The BHI is intended to be the first longitudinal wellbeing index specifically designed to measure the impact of extraction-proof cooperative models on Black community happiness, economic security, and social cohesion. RFL shall ensure the instrument meets peer-review standards for publication in top-tier journals.

7. Phi Trilogy

RFL commits to publishing a three-paper series documenting the Upstream Constant (Φ) across three regimes of economic organization. Wesley Bertil shall be named co-author on all three papers. SAKALA provides operational data; RFL provides the academic infrastructure and publication pathway.

8. Replication Framework

RFL shall develop a documented, publishable framework for replicating the SAKALA cooperative model in other contexts, including but not limited to CARICOM member states, African diaspora communities, and West African nations.

The Replication Framework shall be jointly owned by both Parties. Communities or organizations that adopt the SAKALA model through the Replication Framework shall implement their own 1% research surcharge directed to RFL, creating a self-sustaining research funding mechanism that scales with cooperative adoption.

9. Data Ethics

All research conducted under this partnership shall comply with IRB-equivalent ethical review standards, whether or not formal IRB approval is required by the Parties' respective institutions. The following principles shall govern all data use:

Youth data shall be anonymized in all publications and external presentations.

Informed consent shall be obtained from all BHI participants prior to data collection.

Participants retain the right to withdraw from BHI surveys at any time without consequence to their SAKALA membership or cooperative benefits.

Neither Party shall sell, license, or transfer individual-level data to third parties without the express written consent of the other Party.

10. Termination

Either Party may terminate this LOI upon six (6) months written notice to the other Party. The extended notice period reflects the longer-term nature of research commitments.

Survival of Research Obligations: Active research projects at the time of termination shall continue to completion and publication regardless of termination. Neither Party may withdraw co-authorship from papers in progress at the time of termination.

Survival of Financial Obligations: The 1% Research Surcharge obligation shall survive termination for the remaining duration of any active grant funded under this partnership. Upon expiration of all such grants, the surcharge obligation shall cease.

11. General Provisions

This LOI expresses the intent of the Parties to collaborate in good faith toward the objectives described herein. Except for obligations of confidentiality and data ethics implied herein, this LOI is non-binding. No Party shall be liable to the other for failure to reach a definitive agreement. The Parties intend to negotiate a binding Memorandum of Understanding within ninety (90) days of execution of this LOI.
