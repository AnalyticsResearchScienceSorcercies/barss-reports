What It Is

The Harvest Box (Bwat Rekolt) is the world's first cooperative subscription box... $90 per quarter, delivered to your members' doors. Every box is packed with Haiti-grown moringa powder, castor oil, ground coffee, spice blends, honey, dried mango, and seasonal produce... all cultivated by young Haitians on SAKALA's 25 agricultural franchise sites across 7 departments. Every subscription directly employs one youth for one quarter. No middlemen. No charity. Real products, real jobs, real impact.

What Your Church Gets

  • 5% of every subscription from your congregation goes straight to your Haiti mission fund
  • Free sample Harvest Box for you, Pastor... taste it before you endorse it
  • Quarterly Harvest Day events at your church... cooking demos, youth video updates, cultural celebration. We organize it, you host it
  • Named youth sponsorship cards for every subscribing member... they know exactly who they're supporting
  • Recognition on SAKALA's Partner Church Wall... online and physically displayed in Haiti

What We Ask

  • One Sunday per quarter: permission to set up a table after service with samples and sign-up forms
  • A brief pulpit announcement: we write it, you read it... 45 seconds, tops
  • Optional... become a Pickup Hub: accept bulk deliveries, members pick up at church. Your church earns an additional $2 per box handling fee
