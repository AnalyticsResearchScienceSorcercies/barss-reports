The Problem: Why Aid Failed

$3.085 billion. 16 years. 1,282 awards. 1.3% reached Haiti.

Haiti received $3,085,322,283 from USAID between fiscal years 2010 and 2026. That figure comes from a forensic analysis of 1,282 individual awards extracted from USASpending.gov, the U.S. federal government's official spending database. Every dollar traced. Every recipient classified.

The result is the single most damning indictment of the international aid model ever assembled from primary federal data.

Φ = 0.987. 98.7% of all USAID Haiti funds were captured by intermediaries. The amount confirmed as reaching Haitian organizations: $40,097,516 ... or 1.3%.
