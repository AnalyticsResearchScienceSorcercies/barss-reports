Executive Summary

This report analyzes money flows into and through Haiti, documenting systematic extraction patterns that explain why decades of international aid have failed to produce sustainable development or democratic stability. The analysis reveals that election funding ($137M estimated) is a fraction of annual security, humanitarian, and aid spending, yet elections remain "materially impossible" due to lack of guaranteed funding.

Key Finding

Applying EEDTM analysis, we calculate a Theta of 0.86 for USAID Haiti aid (only 14% reaches local frontline), consistent with the extraction coefficient documented across 20 BARSS cases.

Whether through 1825 debt bondage, 2010 earthquake "aid," or 2025 security support, Haiti consistently experiences Theta ~0.80–0.86 extraction. The mechanism changes; the mathematics remain constant.
