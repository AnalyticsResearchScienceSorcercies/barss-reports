Project Summary

SAKALA × BARSS Relaunch is a cooperative agricultural franchise system in Haiti. Each franchise costs $3,600 to launch, employs 10 youth ages 16-24, and becomes financially self-sustaining by Month 5. Youth own 40-51% of the cooperative from Day 1. Operations are guided by a 147-document playbook covering every standard operating procedure from daily task schedules to quality control to financial reporting. All funds are held in a blockchain-transparent trust where every dollar is traceable.

The $75,000 grant funds 20 franchise atoms across Haiti. Each atom generates $25,164 in Year 1 revenue... a 671% return on investment. After Month 5, no franchise requires external funding again. The grant creates permanent employment, not temporary programs.

SAKALA currently operates 25 sites across 7 departments with 310 youth. The franchise model formalizes and scales what 20 years of community work built. Ten institutional partners are confirmed including the University of the West Indies (certification), SOIL Haiti (agricultural research), Makouti Agro Enterprise (livestock training), and others.

Project Description

SAKALA × BARSS Relaunch operates 25 sites across 7 Haitian departments with 310 youth. The cooperative franchise model costs $3,600 per unit, employs 10 youth, and becomes self-sustaining by Month 5. This grant funds four new franchise atoms (40 youth) plus processing equipment and agricultural research infrastructure.

Each franchise produces moringa powder, peanut butter, hot sauce, honey, dried herbs, and seasonal products through a cooperative system where youth own 40-51% from Day 1. Products are sold locally and through a diaspora subscription box (Harvest Box, $90/quarter). A joint crop trial with SOIL Haiti (111 experimental units) generates agricultural research data collected via KoboToolbox and monitored remotely.

Sister Involvement

The Primary Sister Contact oversees franchise operations and youth formation at the Cité Soleil site, working alongside Daniel Tillias and the 25 franchise youth. Additional sisters provide community liaison in partnership communities, spiritual accompaniment, and quarterly reporting to the Hilton Fund. RJM sisters have been present in Haiti since 1997 and have partnered with SAKALA since its founding in 2004.
