Outdoor Zones

GARDEN — Moringa Polyculture~400-600 m²

All herbs, leafy greens, and peppers moved indoor. The garden simplifies to a 4-layer polyculture under moringa canopy:

LayerCropCycleHarvest Box?Why
CanopyMoringa trees (existing 100+ plus 50-100 new)Continuous leaf harvestYES — powder, teaAnchor crop. More trees = more powder revenue.
MiddlePigeon peas (pwa kongo)90-120 daysYES — dried peasNitrogen fixer. FEEDS the moringa. Staple food.
GroundSweet potato vines90-120 daysYES — leaves (greens)Tubers = local market + rabbit feed. Leaves = box item. Suppresses weeds.
GroundJoumou (pumpkin/squash)90-120 daysYES — cultural productEvery Haitian eats soup joumou Jan 1. December box with joumou = identity.
FillHot peppers (overflow from indoor)ContinuousYES — sauce piquanteAny gap in the polyculture gets a pepper plant.

All 60-120 day cycles. No multi-year waits. The legumes feed nitrogen to the moringa. The vines cover bare ground. Everything produces for the Harvest Box or local market.

Setup cost: ~$50-75 (pigeon pea seeds, sweet potato cuttings, joumou seeds — cheap local inputs)

PERIMETER — Bamboo Grove (Soil Remediation)~15-20 m² (fence line)
  • 5-10 Guadua angustifolia clumps along the compound perimeter/fence line
  • Planted directly in contaminated ground (not containers) — bamboo absorbs lead, cadmium, arsenic through roots
  • Minimum 3m from moringa trees — no root competition, no shade conflict in Years 1-2
  • Year 3+: some afternoon shade on eastern edge — managed by selective culm harvest

Triple function

FunctionTimelineValue
Wind breakYear 1+Protects polyculture from wind damage
Soil remediationYears 3-10Heavy metals absorbed from dump soil, site gets cleaner
Charcoal + constructionYear 2-3 (charcoal), Year 3-5 (construction)Future feedstock for kiln, building material for new structures

Setup cost: $0 (cuttings from wild Bambusa vulgaris stands or from Jupille/Bambou Facile)

NEW STRUCTURE — Rabbit Hutches (DIY Build)~10-15 m²
  • Built by youth — wood frame, wire mesh walls, zinc roofing, shade cloth
  • 4-tier hutch wall: 12 cages in ~48 sq ft footprint
  • Located against existing wall or in shaded corner of compound
  • Manure collection tray below — drops into compost pile or BSF bin

  • Feed: moringa leaves (30-40% of diet) + sweet potato vines + garden scraps + purchased supplement

Build cost: $200-300 (wood, wire mesh, zinc sheets, hardware)

Build time: 1-2 days with 4-5 youth
