Week 2: Train (Days 8-14)

Theme: Every youth learns their role, the standards, and the systems.

FOOD SAFETY + HYGIENE
Dan + all 25 youth (morning session)
DAY 8
  • Handwashing protocol. When, how, why. Signs posted at every wash station.
  • Processing area sanitation. Pre-processing and post-processing cleaning checklist walkthrough.
  • PPE introduction. Gloves for processing, boots for field work, hairnets for food handling. (Even before equipment arrives... establish the HABIT.)
  • Temperature awareness. Cold storage principles. Why moringa must be <10% moisture. Why sauce pH must be <4.6.
  • Practice: Every youth washes hands properly, demonstrates putting on PPE, cleans a processing surface.
Deliverable: Food Safety Plan posted on site (Kreyòl). Handwashing signs at stations. Every youth signed off on hygiene training.
PRODUCT QUALITY STANDARDS
Dan + Processors + QC
DAY 9
  • Moringa grading: Bright green = A, faded = B, brown = reject. Moisture target <10%. Practice grading with actual moringa from the nursery.
  • Hot sauce standards: Color consistency, heat level, pH <4.6 rule (explain why... botulism prevention).
  • Mamba standards: Smooth texture, golden brown color, no grittiness, no burnt flavor.
  • Honey standards: Moisture <18.6%, color grading, crystallization documentation.
  • Batch numbering system: Teach the format: [PRODUCT]-[YYYYMM]-[###]. Every batch gets a number. No exceptions.
  • Practice: Grade actual moringa samples. Assign batch numbers to imaginary batches.
Deliverable: Product Grading Standards poster (Kreyòl, with color photos of A/B/reject). Batch Number System posted.
FINANCIAL SYSTEMS
Dan + Finance Lead + Ops Coordinator

Week 3: Sharpen (Days 15-21)

Theme: Practice runs with real output. Fix what breaks. Prepare for launch.

MORINGA PROCESSING DRY RUN
Moringa Lead + Processors + QC
DAY 15
  • Harvest moringa from existing trees. Use whatever drying capacity exists (solar dryers, shade drying, racks).
  • Process through full chain: harvest → wash → dry → grind (manual) → weigh → package (even in temporary bags) → label (handwritten batch number) → QC inspection.
  • Time each step. How long does it take to process 1 kg of moringa powder with current equipment?
  • Grade the output. A, B, or reject? Be honest.
  • Log everything in KoboToolbox: Harvest log + processing batch record + finished goods entry.
Deliverable: First batch of moringa powder produced under franchise standards. Batch #MOR-[YYYYMM]-001. Processing time documented. Grade assigned. KoboToolbox entries submitted.
MAMBA + SAUCE DRY RUN / HYDROPONICS SETUP
Split teams
DAY 16

Morning: Processing Team (Mamba + Sauce Leads + QC)

  • Mamba batch: Roast peanuts (purchased small quantity for test) → grind → cook → jar (any containers) → label → QC. Batch #MAN-[YYYYMM]-001.
  • Sauce batch: Peppers from site (if available) → prep → cook → bottle → label → QC. Batch #SOS-[YYYYMM]-001.
  • Grade outputs. Taste test. Consistency check. Would you sell this?
  • Log everything in KoboToolbox.

Afternoon: Hydroponics Team (2 youth + Dan)

  • Kratky method introduction. Explain the concept: plants grow in nutrient solution, no electricity, no pumps, no soil. Zero-input after setup. Perfect for contaminated soil sites where raised beds are the only option.
  • Build 3 demo units from available materials (plastic bottles, buckets, or containers already on site). Cut holes for net cups (or improvise with plastic mesh). Fill with water + nutrient solution (can use diluted compost tea as starter if commercial nutrients not yet purchased).
  • Plant leafy greens (lettuce, bok choy, basil, spinach) — fastest Kratky crops, harvestable in 30-45 days.
  • Teach: Water level management (the air gap is the key — roots need oxygen). pH awareness. When to add nutrients. How to check root health.
  • Place units in a location with 6+ hours sunlight, protected from heavy rain (under shade house overhang or classroom window).
  • Assign 2 youth as Hydro Leads. Their job for the remaining countdown: check units daily, document growth, photograph roots weekly.
Deliverable: First mamba + sauce batches. 3 Kratky hydroponic demo units built and planted. 2 Hydro Leads assigned. This is SOIL Track B (hydroponics) in miniature — proof the youth can do it before the full 15-unit research trial.
