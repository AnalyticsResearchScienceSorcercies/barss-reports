Guadua angustifolia is the gold standard for tropical bamboo construction... tensile strength comparable to steel, harvestable in 3-5 years, self-propagating. Jupille has the cuttings. SAKALA has the sites. The gap between a bamboo charcoal operation and a bamboo construction industry is one species.

Part IV

The Goldmine

PREBHA's vision deployed at cooperative scale
344 franchise sites × perimeter bamboo + cooperative bamboo forest.

Every SAKALA franchise site plants bamboo on its perimeter. Erosion control, windbreak, privacy, and harvestable asset. That alone is 344 bamboo perimeters across 7 departments. But it does not stop there.

National reforestation program. Haiti has less than 2% of its original forest cover. Every reforestation plan for the last 30 years has failed because it depended on government agencies or NGOs that leave. SAKALA does not leave. The cooperatives own the land. The youth own the bamboo. Jupille's PREBHA species are planted by people who have an economic incentive to keep them alive.

INBAR membership for Haiti. The International Network for Bamboo and Rattan has 48 member states. Haiti is not one of them. A national bamboo cooperative with PREBHA species data, managed stands across 7 departments, and carbon credit certification is the application that gets Haiti in.

Carbon credits: $300,000-$800,000/year at 1,000 hectares. Gold Standard voluntary carbon credits for reforestation. Every hectare of bamboo replacing deforested hillside generates measurable carbon sequestration. Across the network, this is real money before selling a single bamboo pole.

Bamboo construction material replacing $50-100M in imported building supplies. Haiti imports virtually all its construction lumber. Guadua angustifolia, at construction grade by Year 3-5, is a domestic replacement. 344 cooperative sites producing Guadua poles is not a pilot. It is a supply chain.

Haiti goes from <1% forest to measurable reforestation. Not because of a government program. Not because of an NGO grant cycle. Because 3,440 cooperative members have an economic incentive to plant and maintain bamboo stands. Jupille's PREBHA vision... deployed at the only scale that matters.

And it starts with cuttings.
