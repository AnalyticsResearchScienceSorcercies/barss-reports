Part I

Where SAKALA Is Today

30 rabbits in tire cages. Self-taught. Ready for the real thing.

SAKALA has 30 rabbits at Site 0 in Cite Soleil. The youth built tire cages from recycled materials. They manage breeding, feeding, and basic husbandry... all self-taught. Revenue from local meat sales: $350-450/month. It works. But it is amateur-hour compared to what Makouti has built.

There is no formal breeding program. No genetic selection. No veterinary protocol beyond basic observation. No certification of any kind. No value-added processing. The rabbits produce meat that sells locally, and that is the ceiling without a partner who has built what we have not.

The Rabbit Jerky Opportunity

One global competitor. $117/lb. We enter at $10-12/bag.

There is currently ONE rabbit jerky producer operating at any commercial scale globally: Rickey's World Famous Jerky, selling at approximately $117 per pound. That is not a typo. One hundred seventeen dollars per pound. Boutique, exotic, novelty pricing.

SAKALA enters this market at $10-12 per bag. Moringa-fed, organic, cooperative-produced rabbit jerky in every Harvest Box shipped to 975,000 mapped diaspora consumers. The margin structure is extraordinary:
