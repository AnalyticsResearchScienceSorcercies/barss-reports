Part II

The Reparations Connection

You argue the case. We built the math. And the cooperative.

Sir Hilary Beckles chairs the CARICOM Reparations Commission. For over a decade, the Commission has built the moral, historical, and legal case for reparatory justice across the Caribbean. That case is powerful. What it has lacked is a quantitative forensic framework that traces extraction to specific perpetrators with specific dollar amounts.

BARSS provides that framework. The Elite Extraction with Differential Targeting Model (EEDTM) quantifies extraction across 20 cases, 4 continents, and 200 years. The core finding: the Theta Constant (~0.80) shows elite institutions capture approximately 80% of extracted value regardless of time, place, or mechanism.

The CRC tells the world what was taken. EEDTM tells the world by whom, how much, and where the money went. SAKALA is the extraction-proof structure that ensures reparatory investment does not get re-extracted.

SAKALA is the practical answer to the question the CRC keeps getting asked: "If reparations are paid, how do you ensure the money reaches the people?" A cooperative franchise model with 40-51% youth ownership, transparent financial flows, and university-certified skills is the answer. No middlemen. No leakage. No re-extraction.

The CUCAER alignment: The Caribbean Union of Cooperative Agricultural Extension and Research (CUCAER) has identified food security as its primary mandate. Haiti imports $5 billion in food annually. SAKALA's 344-franchise agricultural network directly addresses this... certified by UWI, aligned with CUCAER priorities, producing publishable research from every site.
