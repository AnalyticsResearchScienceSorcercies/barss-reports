SAKALA × BARSS Relaunch — Center for Global Africa

Letter of Intent

Cooperative Trade Corridor Introduction & Grand Blue Line Partnership
Non-Binding Letter of Intent
This Letter of Intent ("LOI") is entered into as of the date last signed below, by and between SAKALA × BARSS Relaunch, a cooperative social enterprise represented by Daniel Tillias and Wesley Bertil (collectively, "SAKALA"), and the Center for Global Africa ("CGA"), represented by its Founding Chair, Ezrah Aharone. SAKALA and CGA are referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."
1. Purpose

CGA shall facilitate introductions to ILA Local 1694 and the Port of Wilmington, Delaware, for the purpose of establishing a cooperative trade corridor. SAKALA shall provide the cooperative product network, trade volume, and logistics coordination necessary to operationalize the corridor. Together, the Parties intend to build the first chapter of the Grand Blue Line, an Atlantic cooperative trade initiative.

2. Term

This LOI shall remain in effect for a period of three (3) years from the date of execution, automatically renewable for successive one-year periods unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

3. CGA Obligations

Introduce SAKALA leadership to William Ashe and ILA Local 1694 for the purpose of exploring cooperative trade corridor operations at the Port of Wilmington.

Facilitate a port tour and cold storage facility discussion for SAKALA's perishable product categories.

Support SAKALA's integration into CGA's African Peer Review Mechanism (APRM) and African Union network for the purpose of Grand Blue Line expansion into West Africa.

Co-brand the Grand Blue Line trade corridor initiative in joint communications and public-facing materials.

Advocate for SAKALA at PAGTIC (Pan-African Global Trade and Investment Conference) and related trade forums where CGA maintains a presence.

4. SAKALA Obligations

Develop a trade-ready product pipeline for the Port of Wilmington, drawing from SAKALA's 14 documented product categories.

Provide quarterly trade volume projections to CGA for the purpose of corridor planning and advocacy.

Coordinate shipping logistics with ILA and port operations as introductions are formalized.

Acknowledge CGA partnership in all Grand Blue Line communications, presentations, and published materials.

Support CGA's broader Pan-African trade thesis with SAKALA operational data, trade metrics, and cooperative impact reporting.

5. Financial Terms
No cash exchange. CGA provides introductions and institutional advocacy. SAKALA provides trade volume that validates CGA's Pan-African trade thesis. The mutual benefit is the partnership itself.
6. Grand Blue Line

Both Parties commit to the development of a five-chapter Atlantic cooperative trade corridor under the Grand Blue Line banner:

7. Liberia Integration

CGA and SAKALA shall jointly advocate for Liberian maritime sovereignty, including the reclamation of Liberia's flag registry from LISCR LLC. SAKALA, through the BARSS research operation, shall provide forensic economic analysis under the Elite Extraction with Differential Targeting Model (EEDTM), documenting $75–150 billion in extraction. CGA shall provide institutional access to the African Union and ECOWAS for the purpose of advancing this advocacy.

The Parties acknowledge that Liberia integration constitutes Chapter 2 of the Grand Blue Line and is contingent on legal and political developments outside their direct control.

8. ILA Engagement

Any formal agreement with ILA Local 1694, the Port of Wilmington, or any other third party shall be documented in a separate instrument. This LOI governs the CGA-SAKALA relationship that facilitates such engagements. CGA is not a party to, and assumes no liability under, any agreement between SAKALA and ILA or port authorities.

9. Termination

Either Party may terminate this LOI upon sixty (60) days written notice to the other Party.

10. General Provisions

This LOI expresses the intent of the Parties to collaborate in good faith toward the objectives described herein. Except for obligations of confidentiality implied herein, this LOI is non-binding. No Party shall be liable to the other for failure to achieve any specific trade corridor milestone.
