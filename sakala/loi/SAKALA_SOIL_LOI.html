Letter of Intent
Compost Supply & Crop
Trial Research Partnership
SAKALA × BARSS Relaunch & SOIL Haiti

Non-Binding LOI

Parties to This Letter of Intent

Party A
SAKALA International, Inc. (EIN 83-3881370), a Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation, operating jointly with BARSS Relaunch LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (collectively, "SAKALA")
Party B
SOIL Haiti, represented by Sasha Kramer, Founder & Executive Director, or designated representative ("SOIL")
R. Recitals
WHEREAS SAKALA operates a cooperative franchise network deploying youth-led agricultural, environmental, and research enterprises across Haiti;
WHEREAS SOIL transforms human waste into rich agricultural compost through its Konpòs Lakay product line, operating composting facilities including the Mouchinette facility near Cap-Haïtien;
WHEREAS both parties recognize that rigorous crop trial data demonstrating compost efficacy in Haitian soil conditions would benefit SOIL's product validation, SAKALA's agricultural training, and Haiti's food security research base;
WHEREAS both parties wish to formalize their intent to collaborate on compost supply, crop trial research, and joint publication;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties set forth the following terms of their intended collaboration:

1. Purpose

1.1 SOIL shall provide Konpòs Lakay compost and research collaboration expertise. SAKALA shall provide franchise sites, youth labor, container infrastructure, and data collection systems for crop trials and ongoing compost purchasing.

1.2 The centerpiece of this collaboration is a 111-unit crop trial designed to generate publishable data on compost-enhanced crop performance in Haitian growing conditions.

2. Term

2.1 This Letter of Intent shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from the date of execution ("Initial Term").

2.2 The Initial Term shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to expiration.

3. SOIL Obligations

SOIL shall undertake the following:

  1. Supply Konpòs Lakay compost to SAKALA at SOIL's institutional/cost rate for the duration of the research trial period;
  2. Co-design the 111-unit crop trial with SAKALA, consisting of:
    Track A: 96 soil-based containers across 4 compost ratios and 8 crop varieties
    Track B: 15 hydroponic units as a soil-free control comparison
  3. Provide soil amendment expertise and technical guidance for franchise raised-bed installations;
  4. Allow SAKALA researchers to reference SOIL methodologies and published findings in academic publications, with appropriate citation; and
  5. Facilitate access to the Mouchinette composting facility near Cap-Haïtien for compost delivery to northern SAKALA franchise sites.
4. SAKALA Obligations

SAKALA shall undertake the following:

  1. Purchase compost exclusively from SOIL for all research trial containers during the trial period;
  2. Provide the 111 containers, youth labor force, and KoboToolbox digital data collection infrastructure for the crop trial;
  3. Share all trial data with SOIL for joint analysis and publication;
  4. Maintain trial protocols as jointly agreed, including consistent watering schedules, measurement intervals, and photographic documentation; and
  5. Acknowledge the SOIL partnership in all agricultural publications, grant applications, and external communications arising from trial data.
5. Financial Terms

5.1 Compost shall be purchased at SOIL's institutional rate. SAKALA shall pay invoices within thirty (30) days of delivery.

5.2 Trial Budget. Estimated total trial cost: $521 – $728. Default cost allocation:

Cost Category Responsible Party
Containers (111 units) SAKALA
Youth labor & data collection SAKALA
Compost supply (trial quantities) SOIL
Technical oversight & trial design SOIL

5.3 Alternative cost-sharing arrangements may be agreed in writing prior to trial commencement.

6. Research Agreement

6.1 Co-Authorship. Both parties commit to co-authorship on all publications arising from the 111-unit crop trial. Lead authorship shall alternate by paper.

6.2 Data Ownership. All data generated by the crop trial shall be jointly owned by both parties. Each party shall have the right to use trial data for internal purposes, grant applications, and reporting.

6.3 Publication Review. Neither party shall publish trial data in any external academic journal, report, or media outlet without providing the other party a thirty (30)-day review period. Review comments shall be addressed in good faith.

6.4 Conference Presentations. Either party may present preliminary trial findings at academic conferences with written notice to the other party at least fourteen (14) days prior to submission of the abstract.

7. Scale Commitment

7.1 SAKALA commits to ongoing compost purchasing from SOIL as franchise sites activate beyond the initial trial. With 344 planned franchise sites, each requiring regular compost inputs for raised-bed agriculture, SAKALA represents a significant and growing volume commitment for SOIL's production capacity.

7.2 SOIL commits to maintaining supply chain capacity sufficient to service SAKALA's franchise network as it scales, subject to SOIL's own operational and production constraints.

7.3 Pricing for post-trial compost purchases shall be negotiated annually. SAKALA shall receive institutional/volume pricing consistent with SOIL's rate structure for similarly-sized organizational purchasers.

8. Mouchinette Facility Access

8.1 SOIL's Mouchinette composting facility near Cap-Haïtien shall serve as the primary compost delivery point for northern SAKALA franchise clusters, including sites in:

Quartier-Morin, Milot, Grande Rivière du Nord, Plaine du Nord, and Limonade.

8.2 SAKALA shall coordinate delivery schedules with SOIL's Mouchinette operations team. Pickup logistics and transportation from Mouchinette to franchise sites shall be SAKALA's responsibility.

8.3 Access to Mouchinette for SAKALA research visits (soil sampling, compost quality verification) shall be arranged by appointment with reasonable advance notice.

9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this LOI upon sixty (60) days written notice to the other party.

9.2 Active Trial Continuation. Notwithstanding termination of this LOI, any active crop trial in progress shall continue to completion according to the agreed protocol. Both parties shall fulfill their trial obligations through the final data collection point.

9.3 The research agreement provisions in Section 6 (co-authorship, data ownership, publication review) shall survive termination with respect to data generated during the term of this LOI.

Notice
This Letter of Intent sets forth the principal terms of the proposed partnership between SAKALA and SOIL Haiti. Except for Section 6 (Research Agreement) with respect to data already collected, this LOI is non-binding and is intended as a basis for further discussion and the negotiation of specific research protocols, supply agreements, and publication terms. Neither party shall have any binding obligation to the other until execution of such specific agreements, except as expressly stated herein.
