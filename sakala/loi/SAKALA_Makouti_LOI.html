§ Recitals

WHEREAS SAKALA International operates a cooperative franchise network that includes animal husbandry as a core productive enterprise, with rabbit production identified as a priority protein source; and

WHEREAS Makouti Agro Enterprise is an established rabbit husbandry operation in Haiti with proven breeding stock, training methodology, and an active producer network; and

WHEREAS both organizations seek to expand sustainable protein production and youth agricultural employment in Haiti;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties set forth the following terms of intended collaboration:

1. Purpose

1.1 Makouti provides rabbit husbandry training, breeding stock, certification, and network integration. SAKALA provides cooperative franchise deployment infrastructure and a youth workforce.

1.2 This partnership creates a bidirectional knowledge exchange: Makouti's technical expertise in rabbit production flows into SAKALA's franchise network, while SAKALA's cooperative governance and data collection methodology flows into Makouti's producer network.

2. Term

2.1 Initial term: two (2) years from date of execution by all parties.

2.2 Renewable by mutual written agreement for additional one (1) year terms.

3. Makouti Obligations

Makouti shall undertake the following:

  1. Deploy one (1) qualified technician to Cité Soleil (Site 0) for a four (4) to six (6) week training program covering rabbit husbandry fundamentals, breeding management, health monitoring, feed formulation, and facility maintenance;
  2. Provide breeding stock at cost, estimated at $3.50 to $5.00 per rabbit, subject to market conditions at the time of purchase;
  3. Certify SAKALA youth upon demonstrated competency, assessed through a knowledge examination (minimum 80% pass threshold) and a practical skills evaluation;
  4. Issue joint Makouti × SAKALA certificates to qualifying graduates, bearing the marks of both organizations;
  5. Include SAKALA franchise sites as recognized nodes in the Makouti producer directory, enabling market access and peer-to-peer exchange within the Makouti network.
4. SAKALA Obligations

SAKALA shall undertake the following:

  1. Provide housing and meals for the visiting Makouti technician during the training period at Cité Soleil;
  2. Purchase all breeding stock through Makouti, ensuring traceability and genetic quality assurance;
  3. Follow Makouti husbandry standards at all franchise sites operating rabbit production, including feed protocols, health monitoring schedules, and facility specifications;
  4. Report quarterly to Makouti on rabbit production metrics, including herd size, mortality rates, feed conversion, and sales data;
  5. Reciprocate training as described in Section 5 (Training Swap).
5. Training Swap

5.1 This partnership operates on a knowledge-for-knowledge basis. Zero cash exchange for training services.

Bidirectional Knowledge Exchange

Makouti Teaches SAKALA
• Rabbit husbandry & breeding management
• Feed formulation & nutrition
• Health monitoring & disease prevention
• Facility design & maintenance
• Market preparation & slaughter standards
SAKALA Teaches Makouti
• Cooperative governance & bylaws
• KoboToolbox data collection
• Financial record-keeping
• Inventory management systems
• Franchise replication methodology

5.2 Each party bears its own personnel costs. SAKALA provides room and board for visiting Makouti personnel; Makouti provides workspace access for visiting SAKALA personnel.

6. Breeding Stock

6.1 Makouti shall donate an initial ten (10) rabbits to SAKALA as a goodwill contribution to the pilot program. This donation establishes the founding herd at Site 0.

6.2 All additional breeding stock shall be purchased by SAKALA from Makouti at prevailing market rates, estimated at $3.50 to $5.00 per rabbit at the time of this LOI.

6.3 Makouti guarantees the health and breeding viability of all stock supplied, with a thirty (30) day replacement guarantee for animals that fail to thrive due to pre-existing conditions.

7. Network Integration

7.1 SAKALA franchise sites operating rabbit production become recognized Makouti distribution nodes, listed in the Makouti producer directory and eligible to participate in Makouti's existing supply chains.

7.2 Makouti producers may supply rabbit meat and breeding stock to the SAKALA Harvest Box subscription program, subject to SAKALA's quality standards and cooperative procurement policies.

7.3 Both organizations may cross-promote the partnership in their respective marketing materials, farmer communications, and institutional reporting.

8. Termination

8.1 Either party may terminate this Letter of Intent with sixty (60) days written notice.

8.2 Upon termination, training programs in progress shall be completed. Certifications already issued remain valid.

8.3 SAKALA retains ownership of all breeding stock and offspring. Makouti retains all proprietary breeding records and genetic lineage documentation.

8.4 Network integration (Section 7) ceases upon the termination effective date, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

Notice
This Letter of Intent sets forth the principal terms of the proposed partnership between SAKALA × BARSS RELAUNCH and Makouti Agro Enterprise. It is intended to establish mutual intent and provide a framework for technical collaboration and network integration. This LOI is non-binding and does not create any legal obligation except as may be agreed in a subsequent definitive agreement.
