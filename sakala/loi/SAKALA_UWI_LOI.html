§ Recitals

WHEREAS SAKALA International operates a cooperative franchise network across Haiti requiring certified agricultural practitioners, trained managers, and rigorous quality assurance; and

WHEREAS the University of the West Indies (UWI) is the Caribbean's premier institution of higher education, with established expertise in agricultural science, development economics, and cooperative governance through its Sir Arthur Lewis Institute of Social and Economic Studies (SALISES); and

WHEREAS both organizations recognize the need for credentialed certification pathways that enable cooperative youth to demonstrate verifiable competency;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties set forth the following terms of intended collaboration:

1. Purpose

1.1 UWI provides certification authority, faculty consulting, student placements, research partnership, and laboratory access for twenty (20) cooperative agriculture certification pathways.

1.2 SAKALA provides field deployment infrastructure, real-world data, and franchise sites for student fieldwork, creating a laboratory-to-field pipeline that benefits both institutions.

2. Term

2.1 Initial term: five (5) years from date of execution by all parties.

2.2 Renewable by mutual written agreement for additional three (3) year terms.

3. UWI Obligations

UWI shall undertake the following:

  1. Assign faculty consultants (minimum twenty (20) consulting days per year) for co-development of certification curricula across the twenty (20) designated pathways;
  2. Accept two (2) to four (4) student placements at SAKALA franchise sites per academic year, for credit-bearing fieldwork aligned with relevant degree programs;
  3. Provide access to soil testing, food safety, and agricultural laboratory facilities at institutional rates for quality assurance of SAKALA products;
  4. Co-develop certification criteria for all twenty (20) pathways, including competency standards, examination frameworks, and practical assessment rubrics;
  5. Issue UWI-recognized certificates to qualifying SAKALA graduates who demonstrate mastery of the co-developed certification criteria;
  6. Designate a SALISES research liaison to coordinate the impact evaluation described in Section 6.
4. SAKALA Obligations

SAKALA shall undertake the following:

  1. Provide access to three hundred forty-four (344) planned franchise sites for student fieldwork, including on-site supervision and safety protocols;
  2. Share KoboToolbox data for research purposes, anonymized in accordance with ethical research standards and a separate data sharing agreement;
  3. Pay consulting fees per UWI standard rates, estimated at $14,000 to $27,000 in Year 1 based on consulting days utilized;
  4. Acknowledge UWI certification authority on all graduate credentials issued under this partnership;
  5. Provide quarterly progress reports on certification completions, student placement outcomes, and franchise performance metrics.
5. Financial Terms

5.1 SAKALA pays UWI faculty consulting fees at UWI's published institutional rates. Estimated Year 1 cost: $14,000–$27,000, depending on the number of consulting days utilized.

5.2 UWI provides laboratory access at institutional (non-commercial) rates.

5.3 Student placements carry no financial cost to either party. Students receive academic credit through UWI; SAKALA provides site access and supervision.

6. Research Partnership

6.1 SALISES shall conduct an impact evaluation of the cooperative franchise model, assessing economic outcomes, youth employment, food security, and community development indicators.

6.2 SAKALA provides operational data (anonymized). UWI provides research methodology, statistical analysis, and publication infrastructure.

6.3 All publications arising from this research partnership shall be co-authored, with authorship determined by contribution in accordance with standard academic norms.

6.4 Research findings may be used by both parties for grant applications, institutional reporting, and public communications, with appropriate attribution.

7. CUCAER Recognition

7.1 Both parties commit to seeking recognition from the Caribbean Universities Consortium for Agriculture and Extension Reform (CUCAER) for SAKALA certifications developed under this partnership.

7.2 UWI shall take the lead on CUCAER application procedures. SAKALA shall provide all required operational data, site documentation, and curriculum materials to support the application.

7.3 CUCAER recognition, if obtained, shall apply to all franchise sites operating under the co-developed certification standards.

8. Intellectual Property

8.1 Certification curricula co-developed under this partnership are joint property of SAKALA and UWI. Either party may use, adapt, and build upon these materials within their respective operations.

8.2 Research publications arising from the partnership are co-authored per Section 6.3. Copyright follows standard academic publishing conventions.

8.3 SAKALA's operational data, franchise playbooks, and proprietary cooperative governance documents remain the sole property of SAKALA International. Data shared for research purposes is governed by a separate data sharing agreement.

8.4 UWI's proprietary laboratory methods, institutional curricula, and faculty research remain the sole property of UWI.

9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Letter of Intent with six (6) months written notice.

9.2 Upon termination, student placements in progress shall be completed through the end of the current academic term. Certifications already issued remain valid.

9.3 Co-developed certification curricula (Section 8.1) remain accessible to both parties following termination.

9.4 Research in progress at the time of termination shall be completed and published per the original terms, provided it can be concluded within twelve (12) months of the termination effective date.

Notice
This Letter of Intent sets forth the principal terms of the proposed partnership between SAKALA × BARSS RELAUNCH and the University of the West Indies. It is intended to establish mutual intent and provide a framework for academic and operational collaboration. This LOI is non-binding and does not create any legal obligation except as may be agreed in a subsequent definitive agreement.
