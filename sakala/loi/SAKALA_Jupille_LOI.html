Bamboo Reforestation
& Species Sourcing Partnership
SAKALA × BARSS Relaunch & Bambou Facile / PREBHA

Non-Binding LOI

Parties to This Letter of Intent

Party A
SAKALA International, Inc. (EIN 83-3881370), a Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation, operating jointly with BARSS Relaunch LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (collectively, "SAKALA")
Party B
Bambou Facile / PREBHA, represented by Jupille Facile, Founder ("Bambou Facile")
R. Recitals
WHEREAS SAKALA operates a cooperative franchise network deploying youth-led agricultural, reforestation, and environmental enterprises across Haiti, with 344 franchise sites planned nationally;
WHEREAS Bambou Facile, through its PREBHA (Programme de Reboisement en Bambou en Haïti) initiative, operates bamboo nurseries and possesses technical expertise in bamboo species selection, propagation, and deployment in Haitian soil and climate conditions;
WHEREAS bamboo (Guadua angustifolia, Bambusa vulgaris) offers rapid soil remediation, carbon sequestration, construction material, and erosion control benefits critical to Haiti's environmental recovery;
WHEREAS Jupille Facile and Daniel Tillias share membership in the HELP (Haitian Education and Leadership Program) fellowship network, and this LOI builds upon that shared commitment to Haitian development;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties set forth the following terms of their intended collaboration:

1. Purpose

1.1 Bambou Facile shall provide bamboo species sourcing, nursery cuttings, and technical guidance for bamboo reforestation at SAKALA franchise sites.

1.2 SAKALA shall provide cooperative franchise deployment sites, trained youth labor, and data collection infrastructure for bamboo planting, monitoring, and reforestation at scale.

2. Term

2.1 This Letter of Intent shall remain in effect for a period of three (3) years from the date of execution ("Initial Term").

2.2 The Initial Term shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to expiration.

3. Bambou Facile Obligations

Bambou Facile shall undertake the following:

  1. Provide Guadua angustifolia and Bambusa vulgaris cuttings from PREBHA nurseries for SAKALA franchise sites, at no cost (cuttings are a renewable propagation resource);
  2. Conduct one (1) day site visit to SAKALA Site 0 for bamboo planting guidance, species placement, and soil assessment;
  3. Map wild Bambusa vulgaris stands within a ten (10) kilometer radius of Cité Soleil to identify existing bamboo resources available for propagation;
  4. Provide ongoing technical guidance on species selection, planting spacing, soil remediation applications, and bamboo management best practices; and
  5. Facilitate an introduction to Conor Bohan (HELP) via the shared HELP fellowship network to explore additional synergies.
4. SAKALA Obligations

SAKALA shall undertake the following:

  1. Plant and maintain bamboo at franchise sites in accordance with Bambou Facile recommendations on species, spacing, and soil preparation;
  2. Report quarterly to Bambou Facile on bamboo growth rates, survival rates, and soil remediation results;
  3. Acknowledge the Bambou Facile / PREBHA partnership in all bamboo-related external communications, publications, and grant applications;
  4. Include bamboo monitoring data in SAKALA's KoboToolbox reporting infrastructure (growth measurements, survival counts, soil sample results, photographic documentation); and
  5. Collaborate with Bambou Facile on joint reforestation grant applications targeting climate, biodiversity, and environmental restoration funding sources.
5. Financial Terms

5.1 No cash consideration shall be exchanged between the parties under this LOI. Bamboo cuttings are provided free of charge as renewable propagation material.

5.2 SAKALA provides youth labor for planting, maintenance, and monitoring at no cost to Bambou Facile.

5.3 Joint grant applications shall have proceeds split by written agreement prior to submission. Where no agreement is reached, the submitting party retains 100% of proceeds.

6. Reforestation Vision

6.1 Both parties share the goal of national bamboo reforestation in Haiti. SAKALA's 344 planned franchise sites provide distributed planting infrastructure across multiple departments. Bambou Facile's PREBHA program provides species expertise, nursery stock, and technical knowledge.

6.2 The parties intend for this LOI to serve as the foundation for a long-term reforestation partnership that scales as SAKALA's franchise network activates.

6.3 This Section 6 represents a statement of shared intent and does not create binding obligations beyond the specific commitments enumerated in Sections 3 through 5.

7. INBAR Membership Support

7.1 Both parties support Haiti's application to the International Bamboo and Rattan Organisation (INBAR) as a member state.

7.2 SAKALA shall contribute implementation data from franchise-level bamboo planting (growth rates, acreage, species performance, carbon sequestration estimates) to strengthen the membership application.

7.3 Bambou Facile shall contribute technical expertise and nursery program documentation to the INBAR membership application process.

8. HELP Network Acknowledgment

8.1 This LOI acknowledges that Jupille Facile and Daniel Tillias are both fellows of the Haitian Education and Leadership Program (HELP).

8.2 The HELP fellowship network serves as a shared resource and trust foundation for this partnership. Both parties commit to supporting HELP's mission of developing Haiti's next generation of leaders.

8.3 Neither party shall represent this LOI as an official HELP initiative. This is an independent collaboration between HELP alumni.

9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this LOI upon sixty (60) days written notice to the other party.

9.2 Upon termination, bamboo already planted at SAKALA sites shall remain the property of the respective franchise site. No cuttings shall be returned.

9.3 Active reforestation grant obligations shall continue to completion regardless of LOI termination, subject to the terms of the applicable grant agreement.

Notice
This Letter of Intent sets forth the principal terms of the proposed partnership between SAKALA and Bambou Facile / PREBHA. This LOI is non-binding and is intended as a basis for further discussion and collaboration. Neither party shall have any binding legal obligation to the other arising from this LOI, except as may arise from joint grant agreements executed separately.
