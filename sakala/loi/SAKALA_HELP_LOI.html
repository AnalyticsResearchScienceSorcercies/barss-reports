§ Recitals

WHEREAS SAKALA International operates a cooperative franchise network requiring trained managers at each site; and

WHEREAS HELP maintains a network of university-educated Haitian alumni with demonstrated leadership capacity, professional training, and commitment to Haiti's development; and

WHEREAS both organizations share a commitment to sustainable Haitian-led economic development and youth employment;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties set forth the following terms of intended collaboration:

1. Purpose

1.1 HELP provides franchise managers drawn from its alumni network. SAKALA provides cooperative franchise deployment infrastructure, employment, and a structured career pathway.

1.2 This partnership creates a direct pipeline from HELP's educational investment to SAKALA's operational needs, ensuring that HELP alumni have access to meaningful, mission-aligned employment in Haiti's cooperative economy.

2. Term

2.1 Initial term: three (3) years from date of execution by all parties.

2.2 Renewable by mutual written agreement for additional two (2) year terms.

3. HELP Obligations

HELP shall undertake the following:

  1. Assign two (2) to three (3) alumni managers to the Site 0 pilot at Cité Soleil, selected through HELP's career services office based on qualifications, interest, and availability;
  2. Include SAKALA franchise management as a recognized career pathway within HELP's career services programming, including informational materials and alumni briefings;
  3. Facilitate equipment donations from HELP's network, processed as tax-deductible contributions through SAKALA International's 501(c)(3) status;
  4. Provide warm introductions within the HELP alumni network to identify additional manager candidates, subject matter experts, and potential mentors for SAKALA youth.
4. SAKALA Obligations

SAKALA shall undertake the following:

  1. Provide access to the 147-document operational playbook for manager onboarding and training, including governance protocols, KoboToolbox data collection, inventory management, and cooperative compliance procedures;
  2. Guarantee employment for all HELP-assigned managers at the franchise stipend rate plus profit share, as defined in the SAKALA franchise operating agreement;
  3. Report quarterly to HELP on manager performance, franchise metrics, and employment outcomes, using standardized reporting templates;
  4. Prioritize HELP alumni for future franchise manager positions as the network expands beyond Site 0.
5. Financial Terms

5.1 No cash exchange between the parties. HELP provides managers; SAKALA provides employment.

5.2 Equipment donations facilitated by HELP shall be processed through SAKALA International (EIN 83-3881370), a registered 501(c)(3) organization. Donors receive tax-deductible receipts from SAKALA.

5.3 SAKALA bears all costs of manager compensation, housing support (if applicable), and on-site training. HELP bears no financial obligation for manager employment.

6. Pilot Scope

6.1 Year 1: Two (2) to three (3) HELP alumni managers deployed at Cité Soleil HQ (Site 0).

6.2 Year 2: Expansion to five (5) to ten (10) franchise sites, subject to pilot performance and mutual agreement.

6.3 Expansion beyond the pilot scope requires a supplemental agreement between the parties, incorporating lessons learned from Year 1 operations.

7. KOREM Alignment

7.1 The parties acknowledge a philosophical alignment between HELP's alumni reinvestment model (10% of income over 8 years post-graduation) and SAKALA's cooperative reinvestment structure.

7.2 Both models are premised on the principle that beneficiaries of development investment have a responsibility to reinvest in the next generation. This shared philosophy forms the ethical foundation of this partnership.

8. Intellectual Property

8.1 The SAKALA franchise operational playbook (147 documents) remains the sole property of SAKALA International. HELP alumni managers receive access during their term of employment only.

8.2 Manager training materials co-developed by SAKALA and HELP during the course of this partnership become joint property of both organizations.

8.3 HELP may reference the partnership in its career services materials and alumni communications. SAKALA may reference the partnership in its institutional materials and donor communications.

9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Letter of Intent with ninety (90) days written notice.

9.2 Upon termination, SAKALA shall honor existing employment commitments to HELP-assigned managers for a minimum of sixty (60) days beyond the termination effective date, or through the end of the current franchise operating quarter, whichever is longer.

9.3 Co-developed training materials (Section 8.2) remain accessible to both parties following termination.

Notice
This Letter of Intent sets forth the principal terms of the proposed partnership between SAKALA × BARSS RELAUNCH and the Haitian Education and Leadership Program. It is intended to establish mutual intent and provide a framework for operational collaboration. This LOI is non-binding and does not create any legal obligation except as may be agreed in a subsequent definitive agreement.
