Letter of Intent
Elephant Grass Charcoal
Technology Partnership
SAKALA × BARSS Relaunch & BonZeb, Inc.
April 2, 2026
Non-Binding LOI

Parties to This Letter of Intent

Party A
SAKALA International, Inc. (EIN 83-3881370), a Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation, operating jointly with BARSS Relaunch LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (collectively, "SAKALA")
Party B
BonZeb, Inc., represented by Tom Stein, Founder ("BonZeb")
R. Recitals
WHEREAS SAKALA operates a cooperative franchise network deploying youth-led agricultural, energy, and environmental enterprises across Haiti;
WHEREAS BonZeb has developed proprietary kiln technology for the production of charcoal briquettes from elephant grass (Pennisetum purpureum), providing a sustainable alternative to wood-based charcoal;
WHEREAS Haiti's dependence on wood charcoal drives approximately 30,000 hectares of deforestation annually, and elephant grass charcoal represents a scalable, reforestation-compatible alternative;
WHEREAS both parties wish to formalize their intent to collaborate on technology transfer, workforce training, and joint climate funding applications;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties set forth the following terms of their intended collaboration:

1. Purpose

1.1 BonZeb shall provide elephant grass charcoal kiln technology and technical training to SAKALA. SAKALA shall provide cooperative franchise deployment infrastructure and trained youth workforce for kiln operations.

1.2 The partnership aims to replace deforestation-driven wood charcoal production in Haiti with cultivated grass-based charcoal, beginning at SAKALA Site 0 and scaling across the franchise network.

2. Term

2.1 This Letter of Intent shall remain in effect for a period of three (3) years from the date of execution ("Initial Term").

2.2 The Initial Term shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

3. BonZeb Obligations

BonZeb shall undertake the following:

  1. Share kiln design specifications with SAKALA under the confidentiality provisions set forth in Section 8;
  2. Deploy one (1) technician for a three (3)-day kiln construction engagement at SAKALA Site 0;
  3. Train two to four (2-4) SAKALA youth in kiln operation, briquetting technique, and quality control procedures;
  4. Provide ongoing technical support via remote consultation and quarterly check-in calls; and
  5. Collaborate with SAKALA on joint grant applications targeting climate adaptation, reforestation, and clean energy funding sources.
4. SAKALA Obligations

SAKALA shall undertake the following:

  1. Provide the site, raw materials, and youth labor force for kiln construction at Site 0;
  2. Follow BonZeb quality standards for all charcoal production utilizing BonZeb technology;
  3. Report monthly to BonZeb on charcoal production volumes, briquette quality metrics, and operational issues;
  4. Acknowledge BonZeb technology and partnership in all charcoal-related external communications, publications, and grant applications; and
  5. Not share, publish, post online, or otherwise disclose kiln design specifications to any third party outside the SAKALA franchise network without BonZeb's prior written consent.
5. Financial Terms

5.1 No cash consideration shall be exchanged between the parties for technology sharing, training, or technical support under this LOI.

5.2 Joint Grant Applications. Proceeds from grants jointly written and submitted by both parties shall be split 50/50 unless otherwise agreed in writing prior to submission.

5.3 Single-Party Applications. Where one party submits a grant application independently but references the partnership, 100% of proceeds shall go to the applicant party. The referenced party shall receive acknowledgment only.

6. Kiln Donation

6.1 BonZeb may, at its sole option and discretion, donate one (1) prototype kiln to SAKALA for installation at Site 0.

6.2 Any such donation shall be tax-deductible to BonZeb pursuant to SAKALA International's status as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization (EIN 83-3881370). SAKALA shall provide a written donation acknowledgment letter for tax purposes within thirty (30) days of receipt.

7. Scale Vision

7.1 Both parties acknowledge and share a long-term vision of 40,000+ acres of cultivated elephant grass replacing deforestation-driven wood charcoal across Haiti.

7.2 SAKALA provides the cooperative labor force, franchise deployment infrastructure, and community ownership model. BonZeb provides the kiln technology, production methodology, and quality standards.

7.3 This Section 7 represents a statement of shared intent and does not create binding obligations beyond the specific commitments enumerated in Sections 3 through 6.

8. Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure

8.1 Confidential Information. Kiln design specifications, technical drawings, production parameters, and proprietary methods shared by BonZeb under this LOI are designated as "Confidential Information."

8.2 Permitted Use. SAKALA may use Confidential Information internally across its franchise network for the sole purpose of constructing and operating kilns under this partnership.

8.3 Restrictions. SAKALA shall not publish, post online, reverse-engineer for commercial resale, or share Confidential Information with any third party without BonZeb's prior written consent.

8.4 Survival. The confidentiality obligations set forth in this Section 8 shall survive termination or expiration of this LOI for a period of five (5) years.

9. Carbon Credit Revenue

9.1 Any carbon credit revenue, voluntary carbon market proceeds, or equivalent climate finance generated from SAKALA charcoal operations utilizing BonZeb technology shall be allocated as follows:

SAKALA: 80% ... acknowledging that SAKALA provides the land, labor, ongoing operations, franchise infrastructure, and community relationships.

BonZeb: 20% ... acknowledging that BonZeb provides the initial kiln technology, design specifications, and technical expertise enabling carbon-negative production.

9.2 Carbon credit registration, verification, and sale shall be managed by mutual agreement. Neither party shall register credits unilaterally from jointly-operated sites.

10. Termination

10.1 Either party may terminate this LOI upon ninety (90) days written notice to the other party.

10.2 Upon termination, SAKALA shall cease constructing new kilns using BonZeb specifications. Existing kilns already constructed may continue to operate.

10.3 The confidentiality obligations in Section 8 and the carbon credit allocation in Section 9 (for credits arising from kilns constructed during the term) shall survive termination.

Notice
This Letter of Intent sets forth the principal terms of the proposed partnership between SAKALA and BonZeb. Except for Section 8 (Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure), this LOI is non-binding and is intended as a basis for further discussion and the negotiation of a definitive agreement. Neither party shall have any binding obligation to the other until execution of such definitive agreement, except with respect to confidentiality.
