What youth data collectors do: Measure height (ruler). Count leaves (count). Weigh harvest (scale). Record on KoboToolbox (phone). Take standardized photo (phone camera). Mix hydroponic nutrients (scale + water). Test pH (dip meter). Each task requires 5-30 minutes of training. The scientific rigor comes from the design and analysis, not the collection. This is standard practice in agricultural field research worldwide.
