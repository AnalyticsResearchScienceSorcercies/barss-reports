Kisa SAKALA Fè

What SAKALA Does

SAKALA se yon kooperativ agrikol ki anplwaye jèn nan tout Ayiti. Nou gen 10 sit aktif, 160 jèn, nan 7 depatman. Nou grandi morenga, legim, plant medisinal, lapen, e miel. Direktè nou se Daniel Tillias — CNN Hero 2019, 20 ane nan Sitè Solèy.

SAKALA is a youth agricultural cooperative operating 10 active sites, 160 youth, across 7 departments. We grow moringa, vegetables, medicinal plants, rabbits, and honey. Our director is Daniel Tillias — CNN Hero 2019, 20 years in Cité Soleil.

Nou bezwen tè pou elaji. Si ou gen tè ki pa itilize, tè ki chita, tè ki pa rapòte anyen — nou vle fè l travay pou ou.

We need land to expand. If you have unused land, idle land, land that earns nothing — we want to make it work for you.

Ki Avantaj pou Ou

What's In It For You

Ou gen 3 opsyon. Chwazi sa ki fè sans pou ou.

You have 3 options. Choose what makes sense for you.

OPSYON A: Patenarya RekoltHarvest Partnership

Ou bay nou tè a. Nou fè tout travay la. Ou resevwa 30% de rekolt la (an pwodui oswa an lajan). Ou pa depanse yon goud. Ou pa fè okenn travay. Ou jis resevwa.

You provide the land. We do all the work. You receive 30% of harvest value (in product or cash). You spend nothing. You do no work. You just receive.

For you: Your land that earned $0 will earn $5,000-7,500 in Year 1. We improve the land (terraces, trees, structures) — that increases land value even if the agreement ends.
OPSYON B: Ekite nan Kooperativ laCooperative Equity

Menm bagay ak Opsyon A, men olye ou resevwa lajan, pati pa ou a konvèti an pati nan kooperativ la. Apre 5 an, ou se yon manm kooperativ la ak dwa vot e pataj pwofi.

Same as Option A, but instead of cash, your share converts into cooperative equity. After 5 years, you become a cooperative member with voting rights and profit sharing.

For you: You go from owning empty land → annual income → ownership stake in a growing business. You're not a landlord collecting rent. You become a co-owner building wealth alongside the youth.
OPSYON C: Enfrastrikti pou TèInfrastructure for Land

Ou bay nou tè a pou 5 an. Nou bati enfrastrikti pèmanan sou li — teras, irigasyon, depo, estrikti pwosesis. Si nou rete: bail ba à $100/an. Si nou ale: ou kenbe TOUT enfrastrikti a (valè $2,000-5,000+).

You provide land for 5 years. We build permanent infrastructure — terraces, irrigation, storage, processing structures. If we stay: nominal lease at $100/year. If we leave: you keep ALL infrastructure improvements (worth $2,000-5,000+).

Risk ou = zero. Pi mal ka rive: ou gen $2,000-5,000 nan enfrastrikti gratis sou tè ou a. Pi bon ka: revni kontini nan yon kooperativ k ap travay sou tè ki t ap fè anyen.

Your risk = zero. Worst case: you get $2,000-5,000 in free infrastructure on your land. Best case: ongoing income from a cooperative working land that was doing nothing.
