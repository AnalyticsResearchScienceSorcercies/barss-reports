III. Livestock Zone (80 m²)

A. Chicken Coop & Run (30 laying hens)

  • Production: 20–25 eggs/day
  • Use: Eggs for sale + protein for staff meals
  • Feed: BSF larvae replace 60–80% of purchased grain. Remainder: kitchen scraps, forage.
  • Byproduct: Chicken manure → compost → raised beds

B. Rabbit Hutches (3 does + 1 buck to start)

  • Reproduction: Each doe produces 30–40 kits/year. Harvest weight: 2–2.5 kg at 10–12 weeks.
  • Annual production potential: 90–120 rabbits/year from 3 does
  • Premium product: "Lapin aux Herbes d'Haïti" — fed on indigenous Haitian herbs (thyme, parsley, oregano, moringa leaves) to infuse flavor into the meat. French-tagged. Premium pricing. Marketed to diaspora restaurants, high-end PaP restaurants, and export.
  • Manure: Excellent fertilizer (NPK ratio 2.4-1.4-0.6). Direct to beds. No composting needed — cold manure. Applied immediately.

C. Black Soldier Fly (BSF) Larvae Station

The circular engine. BSF larvae are the keystone species of the full organism. They convert waste into protein and fertilizer simultaneously, closing two loops at once.
  • Input: Food waste + chicken waste
  • Output 1: High-protein larvae (40–60% protein) for chicken feed (replaces 60–80% of purchased grain)
  • Output 2: High-protein larvae for tilapia feed (aquaponics station)
  • Output 3: Frass (BSF waste) = premium organic fertilizer for raised beds
  • Conversion: 100 kg food waste → 21 kg larvae in 15 days
  • Environmental: Zero greenhouse gas. 30% cheaper than traditional protein feed.
  • Setup cost: ~$50 (breeding cage, trays, netting)

IV. Aquaponics Station (25 m²)

  • Tanks: 2 × IBC tote tanks (1,000L each) = tilapia grow-out
  • Grow beds: Mounted on top of tanks. Leafy greens: lettuce, pak choi, watercress. Fed by fish waste water.
  • Stocking: 50–80 tilapia per tank. Harvest at 450–700g (6–9 months).
  • Feed: Tilapia fed on BSF larvae (from the BSF station) = zero purchased feed
  • Nutrient loop: Fish waste water fertilizes the grow beds = zero purchased nutrients. Water recycled back to fish.
  • Yield: ~80 kg tilapia/year + year-round leafy greens
  • Setup cost: ~$280 (IBC totes, piping, pump, grow media, 100 tilapia fingerlings)
  • Design note: Gravity-fed backup design preferred. Spare pump ($20) kept in storage.
