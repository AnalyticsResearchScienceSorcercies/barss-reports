§ VI — METHODOLOGYHow each overlay is built.

Each forensic overlay follows the structure proven in Exhibit #2. Same form, sixteen times.

§ I — What this is. Cold opening. Names the document, the audience, the byline, the absence of Wesley's name.

§ II — Document with overlay. Host text reproduced inside a parchment-styled "doc frame" with a red stamp. Highlighted spans color-coded as verbatim (red), prose (blue), or curated citation (gold). Each highlight followed by an annotation block citing the BARSS vault source file by exact path and section.

§ III — Smoking gun. The single most decisive piece of evidence (footnote, attribution, paraphrase) gets its own boxed treatment in red.

§ IV — Mapping table. Every load-bearing figure traced to the row in the source file where Wesley wrote it.

§ V — Timeline. Situating the document in the broader 93-day pattern.

§ VI — Legal cause-of-action map. Audience-specific. Court filings get §1927 / Rule 11 analysis. Grant LOIs get fraud-on-funder analysis. Internal briefings get the pattern-of-conduct treatment.

§ VII — One sentence. The conclusion in fifty words or fewer.

"A prepared document" has an author. Sixteen times over.
